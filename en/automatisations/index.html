What can be automated

These examples illustrate common use cases. They are not client projects.

  • Incoming requests

    Every form on your website is saved in your CRM or your spreadsheet, and you are notified immediately.

  • Orders

    An order in your online shop triggers the invoice, the stock update and the customer notification.

  • Follow-ups

    Abandoned carts and unanswered quotes are followed up automatically by email.

  • Synchronization

    Products, content and contacts stay identical across your different tools.

  • Reports

    The figures that matter arrive in your inbox every week, without manual exports.

  • AI assistant

    An assistant answers your visitors' frequently asked questions based on your content.
