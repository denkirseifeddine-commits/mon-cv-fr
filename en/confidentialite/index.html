What I do with your data

This page says what this site collects, what it does with it and who can access it. It describes what the site's code does today.

Last updated : November 19, 2026

Quote request form

The form on the Contact page collects the following information:

  • your name (required);
  • your email address (required);
  • your phone number (optional);
  • the type of need (required);
  • the desired timeframe (optional);
  • an indicative budget (optional);
  • your message (required);
  • your agreement to be contacted about your request (required checkbox).

If you arrive from the page of a catalog solution, the name of that solution is added to the request. The form also sends the language of the page and the time spent on the form, which the server uses to spot automated submissions.

Why

This information is used to answer your request.

How the request is sent

On the site hosted by Vercel, the request is sent to a server function, which forwards it by email to the studio through the Resend service. The site does not save it in any database. It stays in the studio's mailbox while your request is being handled.

On the copy hosted by GitHub Pages, or if direct sending fails, the site sends nothing: your email app opens with a pre-filled message, and the request leaves from your email app when you send it.

To limit spam, the server function keeps your IP address in memory to count requests (at most 5 every 10 minutes). It does not save it and does not add it to the email. The server logs contain only a technical error code, with no personal data.

“Find my solution” questionnaire

The recommendation is calculated in your browser. Nothing is sent or saved.

Your answers appear in the address of the page. They can therefore stay in your browser history, and be passed on to someone if you share the link.

If you then go on to the quote form from the results, your need, timeframe and budget are carried in the address of the Contact page to pre-fill the form. They are only sent if you send the request.

Cookies and storage

This site uses no cookies, no localStorage and no sessionStorage. It stores nothing in your browser: even the language is chosen through the address of the page.

It uses no analytics tool and no advertising.

Third-party services

These services take part in how the site works. Their privacy policies describe what they do with the data they receive.

  • Google Fonts: the site's fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which receive the visitor's IP address. Privacy policy — Google (new tab)
  • Vercel: hosting of the site and of the server function that receives quote requests. Your browser sends it its requests, including your IP address. Privacy policy — Vercel (new tab)
  • GitHub Pages: hosting of the copy of the site. Your browser sends it its requests, including your IP address. Privacy policy — GitHub (new tab)
  • Resend: the service that emails quote requests to the studio. It receives the content of the request. Privacy policy — Resend (new tab)
